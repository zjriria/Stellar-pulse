# StellarPulse

## A private self-care companion for life beyond Earth

**StellarPulse helps astronauts recognize stress early, decompress privately, and build small recovery habits during long-duration missions.**

It combines simple emotional check-ins, zero-medication relaxation exercises, private venting, and encouraging progress signals in one calm mobile experience.

> StellarPulse is a wellness and decision-support concept. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not replace mission medical protocols, clinical care, or flight-surgeon review.

---

## The Pitch

### The problem

Long-duration spaceflight creates an unusual combination of psychological and physical pressure:

- Isolation from family, friends, and familiar environments
- Confinement and limited personal space
- Disrupted sleep and circadian rhythms
- High cognitive workload and mission responsibility
- Delayed communication with Earth
- Few opportunities for spontaneous emotional decompression

Traditional health dashboards often present this experience as cold telemetry. They can show that stress exists without helping a crew member regulate it in the moment.

### The opportunity

Astronauts need more than another monitoring screen. They need an approachable, private companion that turns self-awareness into an immediate, low-friction action.

StellarPulse reframes behavioral wellness from:

> “Your metrics are outside the normal range.”

to:

> “You are here. Breathe. Let’s take the next small step together.”

### The solution

StellarPulse is a mobile-first astronaut self-care experience designed around three actions:

1. **Notice** — Check mood, isolation, anxiety, stress, and tension.
2. **Regulate** — Start a short relaxation exercise tailored to the current state.
3. **Recover** — Build consistent habits and review encouraging trends over time.

The interface uses a warm pastel visual system, supportive language, and short interactions that can fit between mission tasks.

## Core Experience

### 1. Now

The home screen provides an emotionally safe starting point rather than a clinical dashboard.

Crew members can:

- Review a positive seven-Sol recovery summary
- Log their current mood with one tap
- Adjust live isolation, anxiety, stress, and tension sliders
- Receive an immediate relaxation recommendation
- Start a two-minute exercise when stress rises

### 2. Exercises

StellarPulse includes four zero-medication decompression experiences:

#### Cosmic Box Breathing

A guided two-minute breathing orb using a repeating inhale, hold, and exhale rhythm. Completing the exercise demonstrates a reduction in the current stress score.

#### Zero-G Muscle Release

A step-by-step progressive muscle relaxation sequence for the jaw, shoulders, and neck—areas commonly affected by tension and workstation posture.

#### Earth Rain & Forest Escape

A simulated sensory-decompression player with rain, ocean, and forest soundscapes intended to reconnect crew members with familiar Earth environments.

#### Cabin Sunset Wind-Down

An adjustable warm-light experience for mental unloading and preparation before a sleep shift.

### 3. Vent

A private space for emotional release.

- Simulated 15-second voice vent
- Optional text-based venting
- Clear local-only privacy language
- Warm grounding reflection
- Direct transition into a breathing exercise

The experience is intentionally framed as:

> Private to your device, never sent to Mission Control.

### 4. Challenges

Small daily self-care missions turn recovery into a sustainable habit:

- Complete one breathing exercise
- Log a mood three Sols in a row
- Take a five-minute Earth audio break

Completing challenges updates the ribbon counter and reinforces progress without creating competitive pressure.

### 5. My State

An encouraging seven-Sol summary shows:

- Recent mood check-ins
- Stress direction over time
- Current isolation and anxiety state
- Positive recovery language

The goal is not to grade the astronaut. It is to make patterns understandable and actionable.

## Product Principles

### Warm, not clinical

Pastel cards, generous spacing, rounded forms, and supportive language reduce the feeling of being evaluated.

### Actionable, not overwhelming

Every check-in should lead to one clear next step.

### Private by default

Sensitive venting experiences are designed as local-only interactions. Future data-sharing features should require explicit, informed consent and clear governance.

### Useful under communication delay

Core exercises are designed to work without real-time contact with Earth.

### Encouraging, not judgmental

Progress is framed around recovery, consistency, and self-awareness—not perfect scores.

### Calm enough to use under stress

The interface limits cognitive load through large touch targets, short copy, predictable navigation, and clear visual hierarchy.

## Typical User Journey

1. A crew member opens StellarPulse after a demanding mission task.
2. They select **Drained** and move stress to **7/10**.
3. StellarPulse recommends **Cosmic Box Breathing**.
4. The crew member completes the two-minute guided exercise.
5. Their stress score decreases and the interface acknowledges the recovery action.
6. They complete a daily self-care challenge.
7. At the end of the week, the seven-Sol summary highlights improving stress and sleep consistency.

## Demo Script

Use this sequence for a 90-second product demonstration:

### 0–15 seconds: Introduce the need

“Long-duration missions create isolation, sleep disruption, and sustained cognitive pressure. Most systems monitor those risks, but few help astronauts regulate them privately in the moment.”

### 15–30 seconds: Show the Now screen

Open StellarPulse and highlight the calming interface. Select a low-energy mood and move one slider above six.

### 30–45 seconds: Trigger an intervention

Show the dynamic relaxation prompt and open Cosmic Box Breathing.

### 45–60 seconds: Demonstrate care

Start the breathing orb, then briefly show the Earth soundscape and Cabin Sunset experiences.

### 60–75 seconds: Demonstrate privacy and habit formation

Open Vent and explain the local-only model. Then complete a challenge and show the ribbon counter update.

### 75–90 seconds: Show long-term value

Open My State and the seven-Sol summary. Close with:

“StellarPulse turns behavioral wellness telemetry into a daily recovery practice.”

## Suggested Validation Metrics

### Engagement

- Percentage of active crew members completing a check-in
- Exercises started and completed per Sol
- Repeat use of recommended interventions
- Challenge completion rate

### Experience

- Time from check-in to selected action
- Perceived calm before and after an exercise
- Trust in privacy language and controls
- Usability under fatigue or high workload

### Behavioral hypotheses

- Increased awareness of stress patterns
- Increased consistency of self-regulation routines
- Reduced delay between distress recognition and recovery action
- Improved willingness to discuss longitudinal wellness trends

Any health-related claim should be evaluated through appropriate research, ethical review, and mission-medical governance.
